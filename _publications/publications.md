# Publications

This page lists my academic publications in chronological order (most recent first).

## Conferences and Journals

### SynLogic: Synthesizing Verifiable Reasoning Data at Scale for Learning Logical Reasoning and Beyond (2025)
**Authors:** Junteng Liu* (First Author), Yuanxiang Fan, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, Junxian He

- **Status:** ArXiv:2506.XXXXX [cs.CL]
- **Repository:** [GitHub](https://github.com/Vicent0205/SynLogic)
- **Abstract:** We present SynLogic, a framework for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale to improve logical reasoning capabilities in large language models.

### On the Perception Bottleneck of VLMs for Chart Understanding (2025)
**Authors:** Junteng Liu* (First Author), Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, Junxian He

- **Status:** ArXiv:2502.XXXXX [cs.CV]
- **Repository:** [GitHub](https://github.com/Vicent0205/Vision4Chart)
- **Abstract:** We investigate the perception bottleneck in Vision-Language Models for Chart Understanding and propose novel attention mechanisms to mitigate this issue.

### On the Universal Truthfulness Hyperplane Inside LLMs (2024, EMNLP 2024)
**Authors:** Junteng Liu* (First Author), Shiqi Chen, Yu Cheng, Junxian He

- **Status:** EMNLP 2024
- **Repository:** [GitHub](https://github.com/Vicent0205/Universal_Truthfulness_Hyperplane)
- **Abstract:** We explore the geometric properties of truthfulness in large language models and introduce the concept of the Universal Truthfulness Hyperplane.

### In-Context Sharpness as Alerts: An Inner Representation Perspective for Hallucination Mitigation (2024, ICML 2024)
**Authors:** Shiqi Chen, Miao Xiong, Junteng Liu, Zhengxuan Wu, Teng Xiao, Siyang Gao, Junxian He

- **Status:** ICML 2024 [Poster]
- **Abstract:** We propose a novel framework for hallucination mitigation in large language models by leveraging sharpness-aware training and inner representation analysis.

### C-Eval: A Multi-Level Multi-Discipline Chinese Evaluation Suite for Foundation Models (2023, NeurIPS 2023)
**Authors:** Yuzhen Huang, Yuzhuo Bai, Zhihao Zhu, Junlei Zhang, Jinghan Zhang, Tangjun Su, Junteng Liu, Chuancheng Lv, Yikai Zhang, Jiayi Lei, Yao Fu, Maosong Sun, Junxian He

- **Status:** NeurIPS 2023 [Long Poster]
- **Abstract:** We introduce C-Eval, a comprehensive evaluation suite for foundation models in Chinese language, covering multiple disciplines and difficulty levels.

### Composing Parameter-Efficient Modules with Arithmetic Operations (2023, NeurIPS 2023)
**Authors:** Jinghan Zhang, Shiqi Chen, Junteng Liu, Junxian He

- **Status:** NeurIPS 2023
- **Abstract:** We present a novel approach to parameter-efficient fine-tuning that incorporates arithmetic operations to enhance model performance across diverse domains.
