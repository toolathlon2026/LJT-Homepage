# Research Experience

## Current Research Position

### PhD Candidate, Computational Linguistics
**Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**
*August 2024 - Present*

**Advisor:** Professor Junxian He  
**Research Group:** HKUST NLP Group  
**Focus Areas:** Large Language Models, Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning

**Research Description:** My doctoral research focuses on understanding and improving the capabilities of large language models, particularly in the areas of reasoning, truthfulness, and multimodal understanding. I am developing novel methodologies to enhance the reliability and interpretability of AI systems while exploring their applications in complex reasoning tasks and multimodal learning scenarios.

#### Ongoing Research Projects

1. **Enhancing LLM Reasoning through Verifiable Training Data**  
   - DevelopingSynthetic data generation techniques to improve logical reasoning in large language models  
   - Creating frameworks for verifiable evaluation of model reasoning capabilities  
   - Implementing reinforcement learning approaches to guide reasoning processes

2. **Mitigating Hallucination in Vision-Language Models**  
   - Investigating the root causes of hallucination in multimodal AI systems  
   - Designing attention mechanisms to improve visual-language alignment  
   - Developing evaluation benchmarks for hallucination detection and mitigation

3. **Understanding LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**  
   - Exploring the geometric properties of truthfulness in language models  
   - Developing interpretability techniques to understand model decision-making  
   - Creating frameworks for evaluating model reliability and bias
