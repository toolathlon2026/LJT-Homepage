## Research Interests

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with a particular emphasis on:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**: Exploring how large language models can be guided to perform more reliable and verifiable reasoning through reinforcement learning techniques.

- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**: Investigating the mechanisms behind hallucination in multimodal models and developing methods to mitigate these issues, focusing on visual-language understanding and generation.

- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**: Studying the factors that influence the truthfulness of large language model outputs and developing interpretability techniques to better understand model decision-making processes.
